An AI workspace unifies project management, collaboration, and built-in artificial intelligence into a single environment where teams plan, execute, and complete work together. Unlike tools where AI is a bolt-on add-on, this approach gives the assistant project context and lets it take direct action, such as creating tasks or drafting updates.
